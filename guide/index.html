01

Define the rules先把规则写下来

If it isn't written down, AI doesn't know it exists.没写下来的规则，对 AI 来说就不存在。

1.1Three kinds of rules三类规则

Look, parts, and how parts combine长什么样、有哪些部件、部件怎么组合

Rules come in three kinds: how things look, what each part is, and how parts combine. Designers keep the third in their heads. AI can't read heads.

规则分三类：长什么样、每个部件是什么、部件怎么组合。第三类常常只在设计师脑子里，AI 读不到。

Look · tokens长相 · TokenNamed colours, spacing, type.有名字的颜色、间距、字号。
ApplyApplyApply
Parts · components部件 · 组件Each part, in every state.每个部件，每种状态。
Behaviour · combining组合 · 搭配规则One primary button per screen.比如一屏只放一个主按钮。

1.2Component states组件状态

Draw every state, even the boring ones每个状态都要画出来，哪怕是最无聊的那几个

Leave a state undrawn and AI fills it in differently every time. So name and draw every state.

少画一个状态，AI 每次都会补得不一样。所以每个状态都要起名、画出来。

Write each colour once每个颜色只写一次

Like one light switch for the whole room像一个总开关，管一屋子的灯

1.4Where the rules live规则写在哪

A rule book, a sticky note and a price list一本规则手册、一张便利贴、一张价目表

All rules live in one plain-text file, DESIGN.md. SKILL.md is the sticky note for AI on its cover. tokens.json holds only the values.

所有规则写进一个纯文字文件 DESIGN.md。贴在封面上的 SKILL.md 是给 AI 的便利贴。tokens.json 只放数值。

Where the rules live. The sticky note on the rule book is the skill: it tells AI where to look and how to work.规则写在哪：一本规则手册，封面上贴着一张便利贴，那就是 skill，告诉 AI 去哪查、怎么干活。

02

Package them for AI打包成 AI 能直接用的样子

Five kinds of package, each with its own job.五种打包方式，各管一件事。

2.1Tokens

Write values as a form, not an essay把数值写成表格，而不是作文

Like a résumé template像一份简历模板

Say “bright blue” and AI guesses. Give it a field, accent = #1f4fff, and there's nothing left to guess.

说“亮一点的蓝”，AI 只能猜；给它一栏 accent = #1f4fff，就没什么可猜的了。

An essay作文AI picks some blue. Different each time.AI 随手挑一个蓝，每次都不同。
A form表格AI uses exactly this value. Every time.AI 只用这一个值，每次都一样。

2.2Components · props · .tsx组件 · props · .tsx

Hand over the finished dish, not the recipe直接给做好的菜，而不是菜谱

Like Figma's properties panel像 Figma 的属性面板

Don't describe the button. Hand AI the finished one, with its options (props) built in. Try turning on Loading.

别用文字描述按钮，直接把做好的按钮交给 AI，选项（props）都在里面。试试打开“加载中”。

The manual that comes with the parts零件附带的说明书

03

Check the work检查做得对不对

What matters is who does the checking.关键在于谁来检查。

3.1Self-check vs. machine check自查 vs 机器检查

Eyeballing it vs. using a ruler用眼睛估，还是拿尺子量

AI checking its own work is grading its own exam. A machine check is a ruler: same code, same answer, every time. Try both below.

AI 自查，等于自己批自己的卷子。机器检查是尺子：同样的代码，每次结果都一样。下面可以试。

3.2TypeScript · lint · CI

Two machine checks, explained with LEGO两种机器检查，用乐高来理解

TypeScript checks that the pieces fit. Lint checks your house rules. CI runs both on every change, like a security gate.

TypeScript 查零件拼不拼得上，lint 查合不合你们的规矩。CI 每次改动都自动跑一遍，像安检门。

3.5Who checks what分工

Each checker is good at one thing每种检查只擅长一件事

Machines judge yes-or-no rules. AI builds fast. Only people can say whether a screen actually helps.

机器判对错，AI 负责快。这一屏有没有帮到人，只有人能判断。

You你Set the rules定规则
Machine机器Yes-or-no rules对错分明的规则
AIBuilds, then self-checks搭页面，再自查
People人The final call最后拍板

3.6Pull request

AI can suggest. People decide.AI 可以提建议，人来决定

Like Google Docs suggestion mode像 Google Docs 的建议模式

When AI finds a gap in the rules, it only suggests. A person accepts or rejects. Trust grows in steps.

AI 发现规则有缺口时只提建议，由人决定接不接受。信任是一步步放开的。
