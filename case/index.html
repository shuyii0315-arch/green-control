Green Control

GitHub

One page where an AI conversation and a building dashboard work together. The AI checks three sources one at a time and shows where each finding sits on the machine. Then it proposes a plan you apply step by step, and every action stays on one timeline you can undo. Underneath, the page runs on a small ontology, the idea Palantir builds on: machines, teams and work orders are objects with properties and links, and every action writes back to them. All data is simulated.
